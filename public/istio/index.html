The labs

Nothing in the book is written from memory

Each chapter has a companion repository that builds the scenario and tests what the text claims, most of it by counting responses.

Chapters 1 to 4 build on kind, with one script that runs every check and one that tears it all down.

Then on real clouds

Chapters 5 and 6 use Terraform on GCP and AWS, with VMs, HA VPN and two clusters on two networks.
